# Learning Process Guide

## Question 1: Feynman Technique
The Feynman Technique in one line:  
 It’s a learning method where you deeply understand a concept by explaining or teaching it in simple terms, which exposes gaps in your knowledge.

---

## Question 2: Learning How to Learn (Barbara Oakley TED Talk)
Most interesting ideas:
- **Focus vs. Diffuse Mode** → When stuck, let the brain switch to diffuse mode to rewire and generate new perspectives, then bring insights back into focus mode.  
- **Editor & Painter Example** → Creative breakthroughs often come from stepping away.  
- **Pomodoro Technique** → 25 minutes of deep focus with distractions off, followed by relaxation before resuming.

---

## Question 3: Active vs. Diffuse Modes of Thinking
- **Active Mode** → Deep, concentrated focus on the task at hand.  
- **Diffuse Mode** → Relaxed state where the brain makes broader connections and rewires ideas.

---

## Question 4: Learn Anything in 20 Hours
Steps:
1. Start with the basics.  
2. Deconstruct → break into subproblems.  
3. Learn enough to begin practicing.  
4. Remove distractions while practicing.  
5. Commit at least 20 hours.

---

## Question 5: Actions to Improve Learning
1. Use **[Active & Diffuse Mode](ca://s?q=Active_and_Diffuse_Mode_explained)** switching.  
2. Apply the **[Feynman Technique](ca://s?q=Feynman_Technique_steps)** → learn, then explain in your own words.  
3. Practice the **[Pomodoro Technique](ca://s?q=Pomodoro_Technique_steps)** for focused sprints.  
4. Solve big problems by breaking them into **[subproblems](ca://s?q=Breaking_problems_into_subproblems)** (like recursion in programming).
